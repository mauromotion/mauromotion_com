**Rating: 4.5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/sivbyeykt83vg729hr35hp6pr03s)

It’s incredible how much this book is still relevant today, if not even more than in 1976.

I was looking for a way out of the doom and gloom of the Frankfurt’s School critical theory, which literally shaped the way I think since my mass media studies at the university.

In this sense Fromm’s hopefulness was refreshing. But again, it’s so depressing to see how nothing really changed in 50 years.
