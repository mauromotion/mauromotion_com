**Rating: 2/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/t9iw8wvs02l7kf56tv2iivjuq2nf)

I forced myself to finish this, it was more of a punishment (no pun intended) for something I’ve done that I’m not aware of yet, than an actual interesting read.

It was painful, tragicomic, embarrassing.

I get the power of it, and the prose was gripping, but ultimately it made me miserable.

And maybe it was the whole point of the novel.

Probably I didn't read this book in the right moment of my life.
