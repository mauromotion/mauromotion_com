**Rating: 2/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/gaazmxupy47jiipws7wrjew8fdoh)

I think over time I've read almost all of Poe's short stories (and poems), and I love most of them.

Now I've always wanted to read his Gordon Pym, mostly for completion, but I gotta say this was one the weakest of his writings. It's just very unbalanced, many holes in the plot, changes of tones and styles that make no sense, literal mistakes in the order of the events at times that really broke the immersion.

It is well written as usual for Poe, but poorly executed and researched.

A bit of a waste of time, to be honest.
