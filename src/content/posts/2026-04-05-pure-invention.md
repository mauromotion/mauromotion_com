**Rating: 3.5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/e6a6089xx8opuzjw38rinps47bzj)

Very well written and informative. I thoroughly enjoyed it. The argument though, needs an even deeper analysis in my opinion, so I found this book overall a good summary of the major cultural points that connected Japan to the Western world in the last 100 years.

There's also a lengthy section about the rise and nefarious influence of 4chan on the web, which was again, very informative in its delineating the influence of a Japanese internet phenomenon (2chan) to the broad shit-show that it is now the American (and possibly of the West in general) political landscape, that manipulated masses of people via 4chan and social media in general to their ugly interests.

In the and Japan has been ahead of the cultural curve of the world ever since post WWII, hopefully we can get back on track ourselves over here.
