**Rating: 3.75/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/d2tsnn95gtkexct7vi79l4b0uwy2)

I liked it, as in finally some closure and some answers. The characters are much more fleshed out, and it feels good. I empathized with poor Saul, especially. The whole series was brutal, mysterious, gripping. I'm not a huge fan of the first book, but all three together evoke a good story, possibly the best modern Lovecraftian tale I've read so far.

Still, I feel there was so much potential, so much more could have been said, so much more existential dread and questioning the fabric of reality.

I don't know, I might need to read the fourth book, but not now. I feel the closure of the trilogy and I wanna stay with it for a while. gotta read the third novel, I'm afraid. And so in the end VanderMeer wins.nd that's a shame. I'll try and give a shot to the next one, and see if it gets any better.
