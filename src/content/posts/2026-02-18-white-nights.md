**Rating: 2.5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/jb89csa38ectaqfyvbyhp3gau8na)

**White Nights: Story of a miserable incel.**

My second attempt at reading Dostoevsky was definitely better than the first ([Notes from Underground](/src/content/posts/2026-02-16-notes-from-underground)). But still I struggle to find the real appeal. Definitely different from everything I've read so far, but kinda boring.

There was a section of the first story (I had "Bobok" as well in my edition) that literally killed me; a description made via questions, so painful to follow, that it was totally in-character with the protagonist.

Overall I found the two stories in this book nothing special or too original. Nor philosophically interesting, I'm afraid.
