**Rating: 4/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/razid9r13jetoydkzoi5c4p9mfgd)

Well, I made it through.

I genuinely think that no one is meant to "get" Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics at their first read. I like to think of reading this book for the first time as a mapping, getting some bearings around it. Definitely to be referenced in the future. This is clearly one of those books that gain meaning for the reader at their different stages of life and personal development.

The Penguin Classics edition translated and curated by Adam Beresford was brilliant. Lot's of insightful and clarifying notes with clear examples. I also liked the extra bits of translations (alternatives) put in parentheses throughout the text, they really contributed to convey the richness of the ancient Greek ambiguities, in a lot of cases.
