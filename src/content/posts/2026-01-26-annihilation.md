**Rating: 3.5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/qwuj8qnznjjrc3v5y1hcpaep50rl)

It was OK. An interesting idea that I hope the author developed more in the subsequent novels. Very much a modern Lovecraftrian tale, in the themes, in the narrative devices. Not so much in the style of the prose though, which is both a good thing and a bad thing. A good thing because, let's be honest, Lovecraft is quite verbose and redundant most of the time. VanderMeer's prose is quite easy to read,
snappy, but very dry, too much dry to my taste.

I understand that what we are reading is the "document" of a biologist about a scientific expedition, I understand that said biologist has her own issues regarding sharing her emotions in general, but the overall end result doesn't make enough justice to the good ideas of the story, I'm afraid.

A bit dull, and that's a shame. I'll try and give a shot to the next one, and see if it gets any better.
