**Rating: 3/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/yyraavswf1atps55qqml9x1v1144)

It's an OK introduction to the world UX design. It's very biased by the author's experience though, it's not really meant to be comprehensive or "scientific" by any means, this it's clear right from the start.

I often got distracted by the author's jokes or personal opinions where I didn't really feel there was a need for them, honestly. This could have been written in a more concise and less personal tone. More professional, in a way.

So I think for a general idea of how a UX designer operates this is a useful book, but definitely not an in-depth guide.
