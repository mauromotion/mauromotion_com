**Rating: 4/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/hdq4o98jjwm6nvclvruw9loo66hq)

I liked this second novel much more than the first one of the Earthsea saga. I loved Tenar much more than I loved Ged. She felt more human, more well rounded, and relatable. The world building in the first part of the book was interesting, bleak and ritualistic, and of a ancient earthy flavour.

Le Guin's prose is always such a pleasure to read as well, not a word too much not a word too few, she has this power of conjuring something pure in my mind, that I love.

I might need to continue the Earthsea saga now.
