**Rating: 3.5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/fk4jy6ouwbtr3iuquttgmxnnonle)

As I've recently fallen in love with Le Guin's work, I had to give a shot at the Earthsea saga. But I'm coming from The Dispossessed and The Left Hand of Darkness.
So A Wizard of Earthsea was at in between young adults and adults novel. I liked the fact that the novel is character-driven and doesn't bore you to death with detailed world building, too many names and made up events. I'm not in to fantasy nowadays, as you might have guessed.

Nonetheless it was a fun reading, and Le Guin's writing style is always a pleasure, anyway.
