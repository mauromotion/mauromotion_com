**Rating: 5/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/5j3l8h3sv7l2m7d9cl47z4lxo6vt)

I have no words. I just wanna go live on Anarres now. I'd accept all the challenges, to live a truer life, a free-er life, in many ways.
I'm shocked at how much Le Guin makes you care about her characters with so few words of description. I feel like her prose is very terse, but again, very much effective. Not a word too much, not one less.
Also, this is the kind of reads that I truly love. Not just entertaining but that makes you think, and see things differently. They enrich you, and leave you emotional at the same time.
