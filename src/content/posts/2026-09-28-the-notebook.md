**Rating: 4/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/mkqyixt12itxr2smzhg43yh88git)

I had a hunch this was a good book, but I had no idea it was such a good book!

Surprisingly adventurous and informative at the same time, I really liked its pacing. Often the chapters are built in a way that reveals sort of a twist, so they are entertaining even when they are about financial accounting books in 1600 Florence.

It's also so varied, as it follows the evolution of the notebook as a tool to literally expand human mind, from the ancient world to Da Vinci, from the voyages of an unknown Italian mariner working his way up the corporate ladder of maritime warfare in the Mediterranean to Darwin and his detailed observations about nature during his trips around the globe.

Absolutely worth a read if you are even remotely interested in the matter of notebooks and thinking on paper.
