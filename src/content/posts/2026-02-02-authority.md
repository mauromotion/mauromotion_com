**Rating: 3.75/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/nz5sqck0hbd8m25jquzndsuij0qv)

Well, definitely better than [Annihilation](/posts/2026-01-26-annihilation), the story arch, the characters, the world building as well. Although VanderMeer writing style is so odd to me. The first book was way too dry and dull, you can say it was on purpose all you want but still, not a very engaging read because of that.
This second volume is completely different in tone, which is a good change, but somehow I got tired soon of this sort of constant sarcasm, kinda like a noir detective story, but too heavy handed. Some descriptions were too verbose, useless, just clutter, kinda gloating, easily skimmable, boring in the end. But then again, the story was much more gripping, the protagonist likeable in his own weird way, relatable even.

I gotta read the third novel, I'm afraid. And so in the end VanderMeer wins.nd that's a shame. I'll try and give a shot to the next one, and see if it gets any better.
