**Rating: 3/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/tjzi049x4s5zo2u6wxxetc40qz9e)

It was an overall good read, quite philosophical and constantly moving within a grey area of morality; very well done and interesting. I can see its literary value.

I don't think a faithful film adaptation has ever been made. I thought Del Toro was closer to the original, and it is to an extent in the plot, but he clearly sides with the creature, when the strength of Shelley's novel is in its moral ambiguity, to me.
