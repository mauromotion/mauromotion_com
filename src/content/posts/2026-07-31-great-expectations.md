**Rating: 4/5**

![cover art](https://cdn.thestorygraph.com/wam86o0xsjcndw58e2upwmg29itr)

This was my first ever Dickens' novel.

Wow, I enjoyed it very much. So many feelings, so many plot twists. I had no idea it would have been so entertaining. Didn't know it was also such a Gothic novel, almost an horror story sometimes.

It was a slow read, as the prose is dense and meandering, but also smooth. Once I got into the rhythm I found myself really enjoying Dickens' writing.

I need a palate cleanser now, but at some point I'd like to read "Bleak House" as well.
